Academic Achievement – Physiology Midterm Certificate

First Semester, Year 1 MD · Gulf Medical University

Personal Reflection

A

Description of the Activity

What happened — the factual account

During my first semester of Year 1 in the MD programme, I received a certificate recognising my strong academic performance in the Physiology midterm examination. This achievement was particularly meaningful because these were my first-ever college midterm examinations. Transitioning from school to university introduced me to a different academic environment, with a greater volume of information and a stronger emphasis on understanding complex concepts. The Physiology examination assessed my understanding of the topics covered during the initial part of the semester and provided an opportunity to evaluate how well I had adapted to the academic demands of medical school.

B

Self-Reflection

Gibbs Reflective Cycle

Step 1 Description

During my first semester of Year 1 in the MD programme, I received a certificate recognising my strong academic performance in the Physiology midterm examination. This achievement was particularly meaningful because these were my first-ever college midterm examinations. Transitioning from school to university introduced me to a different academic environment, with a greater volume of information and a stronger emphasis on understanding complex concepts. The Physiology examination assessed my understanding of the topics covered during the initial part of the semester and provided an opportunity to evaluate how well I had adapted to the academic demands of medical school.

Step 2 Feelings

Before the examination, I experienced some nervousness because I was unfamiliar with the structure and expectations of university-level examinations. Being in my first semester, I was still adjusting to the pace of learning and the amount of information that needed to be covered. Receiving the certificate was therefore particularly rewarding. It gave me a sense of accomplishment and reassurance that I was adapting well to the new academic environment. I also felt motivated to maintain my academic performance and continue putting effort into my studies throughout medical school.

Step 3 Evaluation

One of the positive aspects of this experience was that it encouraged me to develop a more structured approach to studying. I recognised that understanding the concepts taught during lectures was essential, particularly in a subject such as Physiology, which focuses on the mechanisms responsible for the normal functioning of the human body. Revisiting important topics and ensuring that I understood the underlying processes helped me prepare for the examination. However, I also realised that university examinations require a different level of preparation compared to school examinations, particularly when dealing with a larger volume of interconnected information.

Step 4 Analysis

This experience taught me the importance of adapting my learning methods to the demands of medical education. Physiology is a fundamental subject because it explains how different systems within the human body function and interact. I began to appreciate that simply memorising information is not sufficient; understanding the mechanisms behind physiological processes is essential for developing a deeper understanding of medicine. Receiving recognition for my performance reinforced the value of consistency, effective time management and independent learning. Most importantly, it helped me realise that I am capable of adapting to the academic challenges associated with studying medicine, even when entering an unfamiliar learning environment.

Step 5 Conclusion

Looking back, receiving this certificate was more than just an academic achievement. It represented an important milestone in my transition from school to university and gave me greater confidence in my ability to manage the demands of medical education. It also encouraged me to continue developing effective study habits and maintain a strong academic foundation.

Step 6 Action Plan

Moving forward, I intend to maintain a consistent revision schedule and continue focusing on understanding concepts rather than relying entirely on memorisation. I will also make greater use of practice questions and active recall to assess my understanding before examinations. As I progress through medical school, I hope to build upon this achievement while improving my ability to connect theoretical knowledge with clinical applications. My goal is to maintain a consistent approach to academic development and continuously improve as a medical student.
