Reflection · Medical Expert

World Diabetes Day – Certificate of Participation

First Semester, Year 1 MD · Gulf Medical University

Personal Reflection

A

Description of the Activity

What happened — the factual account

During my first semester as a Year 1 medical student at Gulf Medical University, I had the opportunity to participate in a World Diabetes Day awareness event. The event focused on raising awareness about diabetes, its risk factors, prevention and the importance of early detection. Being part of this initiative allowed me to engage with an important public health topic beyond the classroom. Although I had previously learned about diabetes through my academic studies, participating in an awareness event gave me a different perspective on the condition and its impact on individuals and communities. Receiving a Certificate of Participation from Gulf Medical University was a rewarding part of the experience and recognised my involvement in the initiative.

B

Self-Reflection

Gibbs Reflective Cycle

Step 1 Description

During my first semester as a Year 1 medical student at Gulf Medical University, I had the opportunity to participate in a World Diabetes Day awareness event. The event focused on raising awareness about diabetes, its risk factors, prevention and the importance of early detection. Being part of this initiative allowed me to engage with an important public health topic beyond the classroom. Although I had previously learned about diabetes through my academic studies, participating in an awareness event gave me a different perspective on the condition and its impact on individuals and communities. Receiving a Certificate of Participation from Gulf Medical University was a rewarding part of the experience and recognised my involvement in the initiative.

Step 2 Feelings

Before participating, I viewed World Diabetes Day mainly as an awareness initiative. However, being involved made me realise how relevant the condition is to everyday life, particularly in a community where diabetes is an important health concern. I felt interested in learning more about how awareness initiatives can influence people’s understanding of their health. As a first-year medical student, I also felt that participating in such an event was a valuable opportunity to experience a different aspect of medicine outside the academic environment.

Step 3 Evaluation

One of the most valuable aspects of the event was the opportunity to become involved in promoting awareness about a condition that affects many individuals worldwide. It helped me recognise that medical knowledge should not remain limited to textbooks and examinations but should also be communicated in ways that benefit the wider community. The experience also highlighted the importance of prevention and early detection. However, it made me realise that raising awareness alone is not always sufficient, as encouraging individuals to make lasting lifestyle changes can be considerably more challenging.

Step 4 Analysis

This experience helped me understand the importance of preventive medicine and health education. Diabetes is not simply a condition involving elevated blood glucose levels; it can have significant long-term consequences if not properly managed. I began to appreciate that healthcare professionals have responsibilities beyond diagnosing and treating illnesses. They also play an important role in educating individuals, encouraging healthier lifestyles and helping prevent complications. Another important lesson was recognising that effective communication is essential when discussing health-related topics. Medical information needs to be explained in a clear and understandable manner, particularly when communicating with individuals who may not have a medical background. I also realised that developing empathy is important, as patients may experience different challenges when managing a chronic condition.

Step 5 Conclusion

Overall, participating in World Diabetes Day was a meaningful experience during my first semester of medical school. It broadened my understanding of the role of awareness campaigns in promoting public health and reminded me that medicine extends beyond clinical treatment. The experience also encouraged me to become more aware of the responsibilities that come with pursuing a career in healthcare, particularly the importance of patient education, prevention and effective communication.

Step 6 Action Plan

Moving forward, I intend to participate in more university awareness initiatives and community health activities to broaden my exposure to public health. I also aim to strengthen my understanding of common chronic conditions, particularly their risk factors, prevention and long-term management. As I progress through medical school, I hope to improve my ability to communicate medical information in a simple and approachable manner while developing the empathy and awareness necessary to become a well-rounded healthcare professional.
