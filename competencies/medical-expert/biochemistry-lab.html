Biochemistry Laboratory Experience

First Semester, Year 1 MD · Gulf Medical University

Personal Reflection

A

Description of the Activity

What happened — the factual account

During my first semester of the MD programme, I participated in several Biochemistry laboratory practicals that allowed me to explore the chemical processes involved in the functioning of the human body. The sessions covered a range of topics, including biochemical calculations, precipitation reactions of proteins, colour reactions of proteins and amino acids, buffering capacity, reactions of monosaccharides and disaccharides, identification of abnormal constituents in urine samples, reactions of lipids and enzyme kinetics. These practicals provided an opportunity to apply concepts learned during lectures through laboratory experiments and observations. In particular, the urine analysis practicals introduced me to identifying abnormal constituents, such as excess glucose, which could indicate underlying health conditions.

B

Self-Reflection

Gibbs Reflective Cycle

Step 1 Description

During my first semester of the MD programme, I participated in several Biochemistry laboratory practicals that allowed me to explore the chemical processes involved in the functioning of the human body. The sessions covered a range of topics, including biochemical calculations, precipitation reactions of proteins, colour reactions of proteins and amino acids, buffering capacity, reactions of monosaccharides and disaccharides, identification of abnormal constituents in urine samples, reactions of lipids and enzyme kinetics. These practicals provided an opportunity to apply concepts learned during lectures through laboratory experiments and observations. In particular, the urine analysis practicals introduced me to identifying abnormal constituents, such as excess glucose, which could indicate underlying health conditions.

Step 2 Feelings

Initially, I was curious about how the theoretical concepts discussed during lectures would translate into practical experiments. As this was my first experience with university-level medical laboratory sessions, I was also becoming familiar with the procedures, equipment and techniques involved. I found some experiments particularly interesting, especially those involving colour changes and chemical reactions, as they allowed me to observe biochemical processes directly. However, interpreting certain results and understanding the scientific explanations behind them required additional thought and concentration.

Step 3 Evaluation

The practical sessions were beneficial because they exposed me to different areas of biochemistry rather than limiting my learning to theoretical knowledge. I particularly appreciated being able to observe how proteins, carbohydrates and lipids react under different experimental conditions. The enzyme kinetics and buffering capacity practicals also helped me understand concepts that are important for maintaining normal physiological functions. One challenge was connecting the experimental observations to their underlying biochemical principles, particularly when interpreting unfamiliar results.

Step 4 Analysis

These laboratory experiences helped me recognise the importance of biochemistry as a foundation for understanding medicine. I began to appreciate how proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, enzymes and buffer systems contribute to the normal functioning of the human body. For example, understanding enzyme activity provides insight into how biochemical reactions are regulated, while studying buffering capacity demonstrates how the body maintains a relatively stable internal environment. Similarly, identifying abnormal constituents in urine highlighted how biochemical changes can provide indications of possible disease. I realised that laboratory investigations require more than simply following instructions; they involve understanding the scientific reasoning behind each procedure and interpreting the findings accurately.

Step 5 Conclusion

Overall, these practical sessions strengthened my understanding of the relationship between biochemistry and human physiology. They allowed me to develop greater familiarity with laboratory procedures and appreciate how biochemical principles are applied in medical investigations. Most importantly, they helped me recognise the relevance of the basic sciences in understanding disease and its diagnosis.

Step 6 Action Plan

Moving forward, I intend to revise the biochemical concepts associated with each practical session to strengthen my theoretical understanding. I also aim to improve my confidence in performing laboratory procedures, interpreting experimental results and explaining the scientific principles behind them. As I progress through medical school, I hope to develop the ability to connect biochemical knowledge with clinical applications and use this foundation to better understand more complex medical subjects.
